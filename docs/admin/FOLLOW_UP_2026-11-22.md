## Waiting for the owner

1. **Early dispatch — BUILT (committed, not deployed yet).** Switch `EARLY_DISPATCH_ENABLED` (Settings) is OFF by default — turn it on
   after the drivers install driver app 1.0.1 build 11+ (older apps cannot see an order before it is ready). Decision (b): A driver is attached when the store accepts, or `DISPATCH_ASSIGNMENT_LEAD_TIME_MINUTES`
   before the expected ready time (acceptance + the branch's preparation time), whichever is later. The order stays "Preparing" for the
   store; the driver sees "head to the store — food being prepared"; the driver cannot mark picked up until the store marks Ready;
   cancellation frees the driver; the driver counts as busy from the moment of attachment.
2. **Driver shifts — BUILT (committed, not deployed yet).** "Start shift / End shift" (on duty / off duty, في الخدمة / خارج الخدمة) replaces the online switch;
   each shift is recorded (start, end, orders, cash collected). **No Break.**
   - End shift (by the driver) is allowed only when the driver carries no order **and** owes nothing, is owed nothing and holds no cash
     (net zero since the last closing). Otherwise the shift ends at the office through the courier closing.
   - The courier closing shows the shift(s) it covers and ends an open shift automatically.
   - A supervisor can force-end a shift (lost phone, left without closing).
   - Alert when a shift has been open more than 14 hours.
   - Kept from today: a driver whose app is silent 30 min with nothing to carry goes off duty automatically.
   - Built details: unsettled = delivered orders the closing would settle **or cannot settle yet** (e.g. driver without a commission
     rate) — both block the driver's own End shift; loans do not. "Stop new orders" is in the app's shift card. The 14-hour warning is a
     red badge on the driver card (staff alerts in this system are order-based). Older driver apps keep their online switch; it opens a
     shift, and the closing ends it.
2b. **Driver app round 2 — DECIDED, to build (after shifts).**
   - **Password:** the driver can request a reset from the app ("Forgot password?" → request sent to Hatly operations, who issue a temporary
     password — no SMS provider yet, so the app cannot verify identity by itself). After signing in with a **temporary password** (set by
     Operations) the app **forces a new password** before anything else. When SMS is connected: self-service code instead of the request.
   - **Stop new orders:** a switch for the driver to finish what they carry without receiving more, so they can end the shift. End shift stays
     blocked while any order is with them.
   - **Orders tab:** sub-tabs **Today | Previous**. When the shift is closed (settled) its orders move to Previous. Previous: compact list,
     current week by default, search by order number or store name, filter icon (status, dates).
     KPIs on both: **Completed · Cancelled · Ignored · Accepted/Ignored ratio** ("ignored" = an assignment not acknowledged in time and
     re-offered to another driver).
   - **Earnings (wallet) tab:** sub-tabs **Current shift | Previous**. Compact view (current week by default, filter icon: status, dates),
     each row shows what the driver earned, with an expand arrow for the full breakdown. Settled orders move to Previous.
     KPIs — Current shift: **Cash in pocket · Revenue (earned after Hatly commission) · Loan · Balance · Unsettled orders**.
     KPIs — Previous: **Revenue · Number of orders · Total time worked** (from the shift records).
   - Suggestions sent to the owner with the build plan (sound/vibration on a new order; push notifications once Firebase is set; today's
     progress line).
