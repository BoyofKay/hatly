## How dispatch and driver money work (final, as deployed 2026-11-23)

**Settings (admin → Settings)**

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `AUTOMATIC_DISPATCH_ENABLED` | as set today | Off = Operations assign every driver by hand; everything below needs it on. |
| `EARLY_DISPATCH_ENABLED` | **off** | On = a driver is attached while the food is prepared (turn on after drivers have app 1.0.2+). |
| `DISPATCH_ASSIGNMENT_LEAD_TIME_MINUTES` | 10 | "X minutes before ready" for early dispatch. |
| `DISPATCH_PREFERRED_RADIUS_METERS` | 3000 | Inside this distance of the store the least-busy driver wins; outside it the nearest. |
| `DISPATCH_ACKNOWLEDGEMENT_WINDOW_MINUTES` | 5 | Time a driver has to tap "I've seen this order". |

**Order → driver, step by step**
1. Customer orders → each store accepts its part (or auto-accept). A bank transfer must be verified first.
2. *Early dispatch on:* the order gets a driver at acceptance or (expected ready time − lead time), whichever is later. The driver sees
   "Being prepared — head to the store" and cannot mark picked up before the store marks Ready. *Early dispatch off:* the driver is
   chosen when the store marks Ready.
3. Who is chosen: on duty (shift open), "taking new orders" on, app checked in within 3 minutes, not full. Within the preferred radius
   the driver with the fewest active orders, then the nearest; beyond it the nearest; drivers with no GPS last.
4. The driver must acknowledge within the window. If not → the order is offered to the next eligible driver and Operations get an
   "acknowledgement overdue" alert (kept, so they can talk to the first driver).
5. **Special case — no other free driver:** the order **stays with the first driver** (nothing is lost or cancelled) and Operations get
   the alert. *Owner decision 2026-11-23:* the system then **offers it again automatically once a minute** until another driver is free
   or the first driver acknowledges — **unless Operations have taken over** (assigning, reassigning or releasing the order by hand closes
   its alerts, and the automatic retry stops for that order).
6. Store cancels / order cancelled → the driver is freed. Driver silent 30 min with nothing to carry → switched off duty.

**Daily driver allowance (العهدة اليومية) — added 2026-11-23**
- Cash the office hands a driver so he can pay stores for orders. It is **his liability** until returned.
- Recorded in **Settlements › Drivers today → "Allowance"** on the driver's row: amount + optional note, who handed it, when (linked to
  his open shift). Each entry is *With the courier* → *Returned* (at the office, "Returned in full") or *Cancelled* (recorded by mistake,
  reason required). Permission: *Close driver settlements* (same people who close couriers).
- **He cannot end his shift while any allowance is out** (the app says so and shows the amount on the shift card).
- **The courier closing returns it automatically**: the closing shows "Daily allowance to return" and the **total cash to hand over** =
  orders' net − allowance (e.g. Hatly owes him 120 for goods he paid, he holds 500 allowance → he hands over 380). The closing's order
  figures and statements are unchanged — the allowance has its own record (with the closing reference once returned).
- Counted in: Drivers today ("To collect from couriers" includes allowance out; each row shows "Daily allowance out"), the closing
  preview (both the side panel and the full closing page), and the driver app shift card. The driver app's round-2 KPIs (Current shift)
  will show it as well.

**Cash in hand vs loans (they are different things)**
- **Cash in hand** = order money the driver collected and has not yet handed over: on cash-on-delivery orders the driver pays the
  store (or the store is paid otherwise) and collects from the customer; the difference, the delivery fee and Hatly's commission make
  what the driver owes Hatly — or Hatly owes the driver (e.g. electronic orders where the driver paid the store). It exists only until
  the **courier closing**.
  - Where you see it: **Settlements › Drivers today** (per driver: today's orders, what the next closing settles, cash held, net to
    pay/receive, with "Close and record the cash received / paid") and **Settlements › Closing** (the full breakdown, order by order).
    After the closing it is in **Settlements › Closing history / statements**. Left-over change a driver reports goes to
    **Cash remainders** (approved → customer wallet).
- **Loan (employee loan)** = an advance Hatly gives the driver personally (e.g. fuel, emergency), recorded in **Employee loans** by a
  supervisor, repaid in instalments at closings. It is not order money.
- Why loans don't block End shift: a loan can run for weeks; blocking the shift would stop the driver working. Cash in hand **does**
  block it: the driver ends the shift at the office through the closing, which records the cash and ends the shift together.

## Background jobs and server load (owner question 2026-11-23)
One timer in the backend runs every 10 seconds (`DISPATCH_SWEEP_INTERVAL_MS`), one run at a time across servers (Redis lock), each step
batch-limited and index-backed: overdue acknowledgements, alert retries (each order at most once a minute), early dispatch, silent
drivers. When nothing is waiting each step is one small indexed query that returns no rows — a few milliseconds of database time per
tick, far below what one customer opening the home screen costs. The apps poll lightly (driver app for new orders; customer order list
every 30 s only while the Active tab is open). If load ever matters, raise `DISPATCH_SWEEP_INTERVAL_MS` (e.g. 20000) in the server env.
