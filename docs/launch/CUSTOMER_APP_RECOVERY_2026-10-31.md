## Findings
- **Banner.** Production `GET /home-banners` returns **6** banners; the sixth/first is the owner's "خصومات اول الشهر" (PRODUCT target, 1981×793 PNG, 1.87 MB, HTTP 200). The app parses and renders all 6 (new test). Why an installed app may not show it: a build older than item 1 builds the banner section only after the whole product snapshot (~3 request waves); a build older than 1.0.0+3 has no banner feature. The image is a raw 1.8 MB PNG (the demo ones are ~34 KB WebP): on mobile data it appears late — upload banners at about 1200×600 WebP.
- **Checkout.** With `REGISTERED_VENDOR_ADDITIONAL_VENDOR_FEE_MINOR` unset, **every** quote (saved or new, any valid address) returns `DELIVERY_FEE_UNAVAILABLE` and `POST /orders` returns `BUSINESS_CONFIG_NOT_SET` — reproduced locally. Production now has it set (500), so this cause is gone. Other real causes with Maps off: a GPS address is refused (`DISTANCE_PRICING_UNAVAILABLE`); an address without a delivery zone cannot be saved/priced (`ADDRESS_ZONE_REQUIRED`). Production's 7 saved addresses all carry a zone, and the zone's service area holds the 30 demo branches (one real branch sits in the other area "ميدان باتا" and cannot be combined with that zone).
